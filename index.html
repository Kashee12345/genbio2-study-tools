General Biology II · Modules 1 to 16

General Biology II Study Tools

See evolution, diversity and ecology happen. Each module has an animated walkthrough that links the ecosystem, the organism and the cell, with worked examples for every calculation, and a Lab with simulators, a practice trainer, a sorting game and real-world data scenarios. Everything runs in your browser on a computer, tablet or phone. No login, nothing graded.

How to use this: open the walkthrough first and go at your own pace with the arrow keys or Next. Then open the Lab: run the simulator, practice in the trainer with its hints and worked solutions, and try the real-world scenarios. Use the tools bar at the top of each page to pause the animations, slow them down, have the text read aloud, or get a written description of each picture. Nothing here is graded.
