MeasureValue

Parsimony steps are counted with the Fitch method: for each character, the fewest gains or losses that explain the tip states on your tree, added over all characters. The "best possible" value comes from checking every possible tree for these taxa. Characters are simplified to present or absent, and the datasets are small teaching sets; real studies use many more characters, often DNA.
