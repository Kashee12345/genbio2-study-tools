Plant Diversity Lab

Explore four plant life cycles and watch the generations alternate; train on n, 2n and 3n, generations and chromosome counts with animated worked solutions; sort traits and features; and read real data on bogs, coal, pollinators, seed banks and medicines. Nothing here is graded.
