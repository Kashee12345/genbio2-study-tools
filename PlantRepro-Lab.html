Teaching model with simplified, rounded rules. Seedling: blue (and white) light from one side shifts auxin to the shaded side; red and far-red light barely drive phototropism; gravity shifts auxin to the lower side; 1 second is about 2 simulated hours. Phytochrome: about 85% Pfr in red light, 60% in white, 3% in far-red, slow reversion to Pr in the dark. Photoperiod: the short-day plant flowers after 5 cycles with an unbroken dark period of at least 12 h; the long-day plant after 5 cycles with less than 10 h; the last flash of a night break decides whether the night is broken. Ripening: climacteric fruit respond to about 1 ppm ethylene and make their own once ripening starts (positive feedback); rate roughly 2.5 times faster per 10 °C; bananas are injured below about 13 °C.
