A teaching model with rounded values. Endotherms follow a thermoneutral zone model: below the lower critical temperature, heat production rises in proportion to the gap between body and outside temperature, up to about 5 times basal (less when oxygen is low); above the upper critical temperature they cool by evaporation, which needs water. Basal rates follow 3.4 × M0.75 watts (M in kg), scaled for each animal. Ectotherms match the outside temperature and their standard rate rises with Q10 = 2.5. Fish oxygen supply depends on oxygen level and on how much oxygen water can hold, which falls as water warms. Water flows are in percent of body mass per day; animals excrete any surplus, so only shortfalls show as losses. Real animals also use behaviour (burrows, shade, migration) that the bench leaves out. Animation speed is exaggerated.
